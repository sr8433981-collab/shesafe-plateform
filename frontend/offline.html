What is and is not available offline

✓ This screen. The app shell and these numbers are cached.

✓ The SOS button is visible, but it cannot record an incident or alert anyone until the network returns.

✗ GPS can still get a fix — the browser does that on the device — but it cannot be transmitted.

✗ Live tracking links, guardian pages and safety scores all require the server.

SheSafe cannot call 112 for you, online or offline. You place that call.
